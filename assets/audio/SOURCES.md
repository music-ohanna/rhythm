## Janggu and kkwaenggwari

These files are from the National Gugak Center’s Korean Traditional Instrument Digital Sound “Single Note Download” service. They are licensed under Korea Open Government License Type 1 (attribution).

- `janggu-strong.wav`, `janggu-medium.wav`, `janggu-weak.wav`: Janggu strong, medium, and weak samples.
- `kkwaenggwari-strong.wav`, `kkwaenggwari-medium.wav`, `kkwaenggwari-weak.wav`: Kkwaenggwari “갱” strong, medium, and weak samples (source IDs 2213, 2214, 2215; originals `Kkwaenggwari_1_1.wav`, `Kkwaenggwari_1_2.wav`, `Kkwaenggwari_1_3.wav`).
- Source: National Gugak Center, https://apis.gugak.go.kr/digitaleum/front/monotone/list.do
- License: Korea Open Government License Type 1, https://www.kogl.or.kr/info/licenseType1.do
